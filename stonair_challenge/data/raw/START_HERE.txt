STONAIR DATA SCIENTIST CHALLENGE — CANDIDATE PACK
Revision A | 12 October 2026

OBJECTIVE
Use the supplied battery-module concept to demonstrate data interpretation,
public-source research, equipment matching and uncertainty-aware estimation.
Required final demand: 100 identical mechanical modules.

FILES
Stonair_Candidate_BOM.xlsx: BOM, CAD geometry and assignment.
bom.csv: portable copy of BOM inputs.
cad_parts.csv: CAD-derived net volumes and bounding extents.
cad_instances.json: explicit placement and parent relationships for 53 instances.
CAD/Battery_Module_Assembly.step: complete named STEP assembly.
CAD/parts/P001.step through P010.step: individual part geometry.

OPENING CAD
Import the assembly into a STEP-capable CAD viewer/editor. Geometry units are mm.
Part IDs match the BOM and placement file. Individual files contain one component,
not the repeated quantity required by the assembly. Quantities are per parent.
The assembly root is STM-BM-6S4P-A; it is a grouping, not an additional raw material.

SCOPE AND TIME
Time box: 8 hours. Investigate P002 (top cover) and P007 (end collector plate).
Propose plausible routes for both, then select one operation per part for detailed
machine research and material/energy estimation. Limit reported energy totals to
those operations; do not present them as complete-module energy.
Treat cells and fasteners as purchased parts. No cell manufacturing is required.

YOUR WORK
1. Build a normalized dataset and your own field/unit/quantity dictionary.
2. Reconcile BOM quantities, mixed units, geometry and parent relationships.
3. Explain plausible routes and preserve meaningful alternatives separately.
4. Search public OEM pages, brochures and industrial catalogs. Aim to evaluate
   five distinct compatible machine/configuration candidates per operation.
   Record queries, URLs, access dates, page/table locations and rejection reasons.
   If fewer are found, document what you actually searched and the limitations.
5. Extract applicable specifications with original and normalized units. Keep
   every numerical claim tied to its exact machine/configuration and source.
6. Estimate scoped material demand, removal/waste and electricity. Separate
   retention from rejection; show equations, units and explicit assumptions.
7. Test important failure cases and explain sensitivity and unresolved gaps.

DELIVER
Runnable Python code/notebook and dependency instructions; normalized inputs;
source/evidence log; candidate rankings; calculation outputs; targeted tests;
maximum two-page findings report. No UI, deployment or paid subscription needed.
AI tools are permitted with disclosure; you must explain and defend your output.

IMPORTANT INPUT LIMITS
Blank fields mean not supplied, never zero. No fabricated-part mass, density,
machine model, process route, operating power, cycle time, yield or energy answer
is supplied. Source these or justify explicit assumptions. Part names express
design intent; they do not prove a unique manufacturing route.
This is a mechanical packaging concept, not a production-ready battery design.
Cell internals, exact terminals, joining interfaces and helical threads are absent.
Cooling, protection, BMS, insulation qualification, tolerance stack and upper-carrier
retention are unresolved. Net volumes and bounding boxes do not prove manufacturability.
The supplied 69 g cell reference is an OEM maximum mass, not a measured assembly mass.

ASSESSMENT
Data integrity 25%; equipment/evidence matching 20%; calculation and uncertainty
20%; evaluation/error analysis 20%; reproducibility/communication 15%.
Supported partial results are preferable to unsupported completeness.
