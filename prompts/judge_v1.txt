- A more specific but consistent value is equivalent. "Northstar Health Plan"

- An abbreviation, initial, or common short form of the same name is

is to rescue clear near-misses, not to adjudicate hard cases. Answering

and well-understood floor. Answering "equivalent" when unsure silently inflates
the score being measured.
